This site ("The Wire") is a static news digest. It does not require an account, does not collect names, emails, or personal information directly, and does not sell any data.

Like most websites, standard technical information (such as your approximate location, browser type, and pages visited) may be logged by the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) for security and performance purposes. This site does not independently collect or store this data.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and other third-party advertising networks. These third parties may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, and you may opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Third-party links

Headlines on this site link to third-party news publishers (NPR, CBS News, ABC News, PBS NewsHour, Fox News, UPI). This Privacy Policy does not apply to those external sites; please review their respective privacy policies directly.
