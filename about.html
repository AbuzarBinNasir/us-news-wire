About

Who writes this, where the news comes from, and how it's put together.

What this is

The Wire is a daily U.S. news digest. It has two parts: a running feed of headlines pulled from established news organizations, and a short original daily Briefing — a piece of independent analysis written about that day's most significant stories.

Where the headlines come from

The wire feed aggregates public RSS headlines from NPR, CBS News, ABC News, PBS NewsHour, Fox News, and UPI. Each headline links directly back to the original publisher — this site does not republish full articles, and every image and story ultimately belongs to and is credited to its original source. New stories are added automatically each day; anything older than seven days is removed.

How the Briefing is written

Each day, an AI system drafts an original analysis piece based on that day's headlines. Every draft is reviewed and edited by a human before it's published — nothing goes live automatically. The goal is a short, neutral, independently-written take on what's actually significant that day, not a rewrite of any single article.
